## Who I am

I am Brissia Benavente, a business scientist based in La Paz, Bolivia. I work where growth, strategy and territory meet: I study how technology, cities and human behavior shape the way businesses expand across Latin America.

I am also a published poet, a critic of contemporary Bolivian art and a journalist who has written about fashion and technology. I do not keep those lives separate. They are the same curiosity pointed at different objects.

## What I believe

Critical thinking first, then everything else.

A good question is worth more than a fast answer. Markets are made of people, and people live in places. Intelligence is collective: most of what I know, I learned in conversation.

## How I think

I start from the problem, not from the tool. I look for the evidence before the opinion, and for the place before the average. Then I write it down, because an idea that cannot be written clearly is not finished.

## Mentoring

For more than seventeen years I have worked in strategic marketing and growth, and I have mentored more than eighty startups in accelerators across Latin America. Mentoring, for me, is not advice. It is helping someone see their own problem with more precision.
