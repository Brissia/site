## Who I Am

I'm Brissia Benavente, a Bolivian growth strategist, business scientist and digital nomad.

My work lives at the intersection of strategy, territorial intelligence and human behavior. I study how people, cities and technology shape the way businesses grow, particularly across Latin America.

I'm also a published poet, a contemporary Bolivian art critic, and a journalist covering fashion and technology. I've never seen these as separate careers. They're simply different ways of exploring the same question: **why do people make the decisions they make?**

## What I Believe

The best decisions rarely begin with fast answers. They usually begin with better questions.

Markets are not abstract systems. They are people living in real places, shaped by history, culture and geography.

I also believe that knowledge is inherently collective. Most of what I know wasn't learned in boardrooms or classrooms, but through conversations, travel and careful observation.

I observe power dynamics not through the lens of morality or ideology, but with deep curiosity about, and an understanding of, human behavior.

Critical thinking comes first.

Everything else follows.

## How I Think

I don't start with frameworks or tools.

I start with the problem.

I look for patterns before trends, evidence before opinions and context before averages.

Then I write.

Writing is how I think. If an idea cannot be explained clearly, it probably isn't finished yet.

## Mentoring

For more than seventeen years I've helped companies and startups grow, mentoring over one hundred founders through accelerators and innovation programs across Latin America and Europe.

To me, mentoring is not about giving answers.

It's about helping people ask better questions.

Once the right question appears, the path forward usually becomes much clearer.
