We have always been sold the idea that being an entrepreneur means having an idea, prototyping it, starting out in a garage and designing the logo at your kitchen table. But how do you picture the entrepreneurs of the future?

I want to share how I picture them... To do that, I am going to introduce two concepts and how they will shape the future, not only of business but of the people who lead it.

## The birth of the Business Scientist.

Just when you thought that becoming a data scientist would put you at the top of the professional pyramid, I have to tell you: you are no longer the sexiest one on the team!

More and more people are talking about Business Scientists. But Bri!!! What is that now? Surely something that already existed, with a new, *fancier* name...

A Business Scientist combines the analytical rigor of a data scientist with a strategic business mindset. Their main job is to turn large volumes of data into **profitable business models**. The big difference is that this profile has no intention of starting a company, either because entrepreneurship doesn't fit their personal goals or because they don't have a CEO's skills and aren't interested in developing them. I recommend reading my article on what to expect from the C-level.

So this Business Scientist puts all their skills into minimizing the business model's risk of failure, applying highly sophisticated advanced analytics models and, in doing so, (in a way) guaranteeing its success.

You can probably guess where I am going with this, right? If there is a profile capable of *“packaging” business models, all that is missing is the counterpart.*

## What is ETA, and why should you be thinking about it if you are an entrepreneur?

**Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (ETA)**. Instead of building a business from scratch, ETA means buying an existing company, taking the reins and bringing it to its next level. It is an approach that blends strategy, innovation and leadership. And no, it is not just about money: **it is about skills, vision and humanity.**

At this point there are two ways to go: acquire a data-driven business model, or acquire a company with a solid base, loyal employees and recurring customers. You inject innovation, new growth strategies and tools like artificial intelligence to optimize processes, and that is ETA: not just a shortcut into entrepreneurship, but a smarter way to lead.

To everyone who calls themselves a *“serial entrepreneur”*: this is the model I imagine you will have to evolve into.

---

Business Scientists and ETA are here to transform what we understand by entrepreneurship. Less risk, more strategy. Less improvisation, more data. Less ego, more collaboration. The future doesn't belong to those who invent alone, but to those who lead with intelligence, humanity and a data-driven vision.

As a mentor, I have had the privilege of helping bring this perspective into practice, building strategies that combine the analytical precision of a Business Scientist with the innovation of an entrepreneur. My focus is on minimizing the chances of failure, not only by optimizing business models but by helping founders build sustainable systems that prepare them for what comes next. Because success is no longer a matter of luck: it is a matter of science, strategy and skills.
