Every founder I know dreams of hitting “product-market fit” and of a user acquisition strategy that runs by itself. But if you are a #**solopreneur**, the story starts differently: there is no army of salespeople and no growth team behind you. There is only you, your laptop and your ability to design a system that grows *with you and in spite of you*.

So today I want to talk to you about this growth strategy: ✨ **Founder-Led Growth** ✨ that phase where the founder is, all at once, CEO, CTO, salesperson, finance and *customer success manager*. It is an intense road, but also the one that teaches you the most.

OK Bri, but **how do I reach my first 1,000 users without dying in the attempt?**

---

### 1. Talk to your users (active listening)

Before you obsess over automating, make time for brutal *feedback loops*. Your advantage as a founder is closeness: you can hear your users' frictions and wishes first-hand. That is your gold mine for designing product and narratives.

---

### 2. Build community, not just an audience

The most common mistake, and my favorite sin, as Al Pacino would say..., #Vanity: measuring *followers* instead of *engaged users*. A WhatsApp group, a Discord channel or a well-fed mailing list is worth more than 10K views on a reel that doesn't convert. Remember: the first 1,000 arrive through closeness and trust. The good old friends, family and fools.

---

### 3. Let AI do the dirty work

*Founder-Led Growth* doesn't mean you have to do everything by hand. Automate 80% of admin tasks, prospecting and cold outreach. Free yourself to focus on human conversations and strategy.

---

### 4. Design an acquisition funnel that is handmade, but efficient

You don't need a $5K-a-month HubSpot to get started. But you do need a defined flow:

- Where do your leads come from?
- How do you contact them?
- What do you offer them first?
- How do you turn them into customers and keep them?
- What works, what doesn't...

Clarity in the funnel keeps you from scattering.

---

### 5. Don't underestimate offline

A founder's best weapon is their network. Go to events, media, conferences and every possible space where you can meet your next partner, customer or investor.
