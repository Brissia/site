### But Bri! What is the SSI and why should I care?

The **Social Selling Index** is a metric created by LinkedIn that measures your ability to:

1. Establish a professional brand (not just a personal one, see the difference?)
2. Find the right people to connect with.
3. Engage by offering value to your community.
4. Build relationships strategically and with purpose.

The higher your SSI, the better your chances of influencing decisions, attracting leads or job offers, making valuable connections and building thought leadership. And best of all: **it is free, and the cool part is that it tells you specifically where you have the most room to improve... in my case, for example: finding the right people.**

## First, what DOES work, OK?

### 1. Publish content of VALUE at least once a week (Factor: “Establish your professional brand”)

You don't need to be a content creator 24/7. But you do need to show your expertise with content that brings real value to your audience. Publish insights, analysis, cases, lessons or mistakes. Focus on content that shows you know what you are talking about.

**Quick tips:**

- Use storytelling and your own experience.
- Share measurable results or lessons.
- Use varied formats, but the one that best suits your personality (text, polls, carousels). I, for example, prefer to write because making videos is such a drag hahaha

🕐 Visible impact in 2-3 weeks.

---

### 2. Connect with 10 strategic profiles a week (Factor: “Find the right people”)

It is not about quantity but quality. Aim to connect with decision-makers, leading voices in YOUR sector or potential customers. Personalize the connection message and be genuine. No ctrl c / ctrl v 😾

**Do it like this:**

- Search using industry and job title filters.
- Write personalized invitations (“I saw your work at \_\_\_ and I would love to connect because \_\_\_”).
- Then **engage with their posts** (comment, don't just like).

🕐 Visible impact from week 1.

---

### 3. Comment with depth and frequency on key posts (Factor: “Engage with insights”)

Commenting is one of the most underrated tools on LinkedIn. A good comment can bring more visibility than a post. Put adding value first, not just saying “I love this”.

**Tips for commenting well:**

- Add your own perspective, an example or a reflection.
- Avoid empty comments like “totally agree”. I do it sometimes and realized it doesn't work...
- Comment on posts by thought leaders or potential customers.

🕐 Visible impact within a few days.

## Now, what does NOT work and is bullsh!+

### What didn't work for me (and saves you time)

❌ Sending requests without personalizing them. Yes, you add connections, but not relationships.

❌ Asking for recommendations in bulk. It is not quantity, it is credibility. Two or three well-written recommendations are worth more than ten empty ones.

❌ Using bots to automate messages. This doesn't just ruin your SSI, it ruins your reputation. Social Selling is exactly that: social. Not spam.

❌ The worst of all is sharing AI-generated content without reviewing it, correcting it and adding your own llajuita, your own spice. If it starts with "In a world revolutionized by AI...." #Bullsh!+ hahaha
