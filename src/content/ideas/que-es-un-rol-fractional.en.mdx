"A Head of Growth is a leadership role. It deals with strategy. It is 70% strategy and little execution. If you want someone who executes 70-80% of the time, then don't hire a Head of Growth. Hire a specialist in acquisition channels or performance or, at most, a Marketing Manager.” That is what [Barbara Malet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/barbaramalet) tells us on her podcast [The Growth System](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-growth-system-co/). And I think it is suuuper important to share a couple of ideas with you, especially with founders of early-stage startups.

## So Bri... do I hire full-time or fractional?

If you are building a startup (and even more so if you are in Bolivia), you are most likely facing one of these challenges:

1️⃣ **You can't afford to hire senior talent full-time**.

2️⃣ **You need senior strategy but also hands-on execution**.

3️⃣ **You have no room for expensive hiring mistakes**.

This is where the concept of **fractional talent** comes in. Or, in its *fancy* form, #fractional CMO, CFO, COO, meow, meow, meow, etc...

🔹 **What a fractional role is (and why you should care)** Put simply, a fractional professional is a **high-level executive or specialist who works with several companies at the same time**, bringing their experience without needing to be full-time. **Example:** Instead of hiring a **senior Head of Growth full-time** while you are still looking for product-market fit, you bring in a **Fractional Head of Growth** who defines the strategy, sets up the first processes and guides the in-house team until you can scale.

---

## The most common mistakes when hiring talent in startups 🚨

❌ **Looking for unicorns that don't exist** How many founders have put this in their job descriptions: 👉 *"We are looking for a Growth Hacker who also does PR, designs, runs ads and has experience scaling B2B SaaS companies to 100M ARR”* And on top of that they want to pay them 2K 😅. One profile can't cover everything. **Trying to find “full-stack” talent usually ends in bad hires and trial-and-error cycles that cost you money and time**.

❌ **Thinking that “executing” is enough** The most serious mistake founders make: hiring juniors to execute (in Bolivia, the classic “hire interns and give them bus fare”), with nobody to **design the strategy**. I see it every day. They want to scale with serious structural problems. A **Fractional Head of Growth**, for example, is **70% strategy and 30% execution**, making sure every action makes sense within scalable, sustainable growth.

❌ **Not valuing real experience** There is a giant difference between "knowing because you read it on a blog" and "knowing because you did it yourself”. Besides, you can smell the #gurus from miles away, right?

Fractional talent usually means profiles **who have already been through multiple startups, who understand what works and what doesn't, and who can apply what they learned in real scenarios**.

---

## When to consider a fractional role in your startup

✅ **If you are at seed or pre-Series A stage and need strategy, but can't yet pay a full-time C-level.**

✅ **If you need to scale fast and can't lose months hiring, training and waiting for someone to “learn” the business.**

✅ **If you want to avoid high turnover in key roles and tap proven talent without long-term commitments.**

Fractional talent **is the bridge between strategy and execution**, allowing startups to **make smart decisions, validate hypotheses quickly and optimize resources**. In another post I will share compensation and fee models (how to pay, or charge, for a fractional role!)

🚀 **Conclusion: the future of talent is not full-time, it is flexible.** I have always said it in my analysis of the #FutureOfWork: the professionals of the future will be able to work from anywhere (remote), at any time (asynchronous) and with any company (fractional). Founders who understand this and make use of the fractional model will definitely have the advantage.
