I help startups and companies with their growth and strategy challenges.

I work alongside startups and teams on growth, user acquisition and monetization, drawing on hands-on experience and working as part of the team. The aim is to sharpen skills, bring clarity on direction and deliver results that last and scale.

## Fractional CMO / Head of Growth

I lead growth strategy and its execution, and get the team performing at a high level, in startups and corporations alike.

### Initial diagnosis

We work together to understand where things stand and what needs attention first.

- One-on-one interviews with key stakeholders
- A review of metrics and funnel analysis
- Identifying growth opportunities

### Strategic plan

We define a North Star Metric and design a strategic plan with concrete KPIs for the short, medium and long term.

- Customer acquisition and retention strategy
- Optimizing teams and internal processes
- Putting growth tools in place

### Implementation

I lead your team, or hire the key profiles, to execute the plan and reach the goals we set.

- Weekly metrics tracking
- Real-time adjustments to improve results
- Support on strategic decisions
