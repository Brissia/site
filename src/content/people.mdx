## Effective Remote Job Search

**Learn how to reach job opportunities anywhere in the world.**

In this program I help you identify and build on your strengths, sharpen your professional identity and create a personal brand that gets the attention of recruiters worldwide. We prepare for your interviews together, and I show you how to build the professional connections that open new doors. You will not only improve your profile: you will be ready to make the most of every opportunity and take the next big step in your career.

## What does the program cover?

The full program runs over four sessions, from self-knowledge and finding your purpose to building an effective personal brand.

### Session 1: Purpose and self-knowledge

- Exploring where you are now and what you want professionally
- Identifying your strengths, values and motivations
- Defining your “North Star”, your professional purpose

### Session 2: Your ideal professional profile

- Building a coherent, compelling professional profile
- Defining your message and a plan for applying effectively
- Designing a training and certification plan aligned with your goals

### Session 3: Preparing for interviews and selection processes

- Handling difficult questions and objections
- Strategies for psychometric and aptitude tests
- Practice for interviews in English

### Session 4: Personal brand and networking

- Developing a strategy for professional visibility
- Optimizing your presence on professional networks such as LinkedIn
- Creating a networking plan to connect with leaders in your industry

## Who is this program for?

This program is for you if:

- You are starting your working life
- You are unemployed and looking for work
- You are unhappy in your current job
- You are an entrepreneur who needs to find employment
- You are returning to the job market after a long time away
- You want to earn in $ or €

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is this program for?

It is designed for people, with or without a degree, who are looking for a job, want to change jobs or are re-entering the job market after starting a business or taking a break. It is a space to rediscover your value and move forward with clarity.

### What topics do the sessions cover?

We go from self-knowledge and professional purpose to building a clear professional profile, preparing for interviews, a visibility strategy on LinkedIn and designing a growth plan. All of it practical and tailored to you.

### Do I need previous professional experience to join?

No. You may be starting your career, in transition or have years of experience. The program adapts to where you are professionally and to your specific goals.

### How many sessions do I need to see results?

Four strategic sessions are enough for a clear transformation in your professional profile and your employability strategy. Some people choose to continue with follow-up sessions for deeper support.

### What results can I expect?

More clarity about who you are as a professional, a powerful and authentic message about your value, concrete tools to apply strategically and an action plan aligned with your goals and purpose. And most important of all, a job that lives up to your expectations.

### What is the difference between mentoring and professional coaching?

The program combines coaching tools (powerful questions, introspection) with practical, strategic guidance. I don't only help you find your own answers: I also share structures, templates and resources that have proven to work.

### Does the program guarantee me a job?

I own the process and you own the result, which depends on the commitment and seriousness you bring to the program. If you are fully committed to this process, then yes: I guarantee you will reach your goals.

### What if I am not sure what I want to apply for?

Even better! The program starts from exactly that point: helping you know yourself, define your professional “North Star” and build your ideal profile, and then design a plan around it.

### Is the mentoring in person or online?

Sessions are 100% online, so you can join from anywhere in the world. If you are in Bolivia, we sometimes offer group meetings in person.
