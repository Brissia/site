**A simple way to decide what to test first**

## 💡 The main idea

Not every experiment is worth the same effort.

The **ICE** framework helps you prioritize objectively, picking the experiments with the **most impact for the least cost**, using three variables:

## 🔢 ICE = Impact × Confidence × Ease

| Factor | Key question | Scale (1–10) | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Impact** | How big will the effect be if it works? | 1 = low / 10 = very high | “It could double our weekly demos” |
| **Confidence** | How sure are you that it will work? | 1 = pure gut feeling / 10 = backed by data | “We already saw signals in earlier campaigns” |
| **Ease** | How easy is it to ship quickly? | 1 = needs development / 10 = you can do it today | “I only need to tweak some copy on LinkedIn” |

📊 **Formula:**

👉 **ICE Score = (Impact + Confidence + Ease) / 3**

## ⚙️ How to use it in your startup

1. Build a table with all your experiments (in Notion, Sheets or Airtable).
2. Score each experiment from 1 to 10 on the three variables.
3. Work out the average (ICE).
4. Sort from highest to lowest and **run only the top 2–3**.
5. Repeat every two weeks: ICE changes with context.
