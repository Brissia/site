## 🎯 The core idea

There are dozens of possible channels (SEO, partnerships, email, PR, events, paid media, referrals and more), but **only one or two will actually give you traction**.

The Bullseye Framework helps you find *which channels those are* through a structured process of exploration and prioritization.

## 🔵 The three rings of the bullseye

### 1️⃣ Outer Ring – Brainstorm (Explore)

> “Everything that could work”

- Start by **brainstorming** every possible channel (20 or more).
- Think of **non-obvious ways** to win customers: PR, affiliates, community, events, referrals and so on.
- Don't judge yet. The goal is breadth.

📍 *Example:* For a B2B SaaS you might list outbound, LinkedIn Ads, webinars, partnerships with ERPs, marketplaces, customer referrals and so on.

### 2️⃣ Middle Ring – Test (Experiment)

> “What might work”

- Pick the **3 to 5 channels** with the most potential or the lowest starting cost.
- Design **fast, measurable experiments** (7–14 days) to test for real traction.
- Judge each channel on:
  - Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
  - Conversion rate
  - Scalability
  - What you learned

📍 *Example:* You try LinkedIn Ads with $100, run a webinar with 20 attendees and send 50 outreach messages on LinkedIn. Then you measure which one brought in more demos or sign-ups.

### 3️⃣ Inner Ring – Focus (Double down)

> “What is working”

- Focus on the **1 or 2 channels showing signs of PMF (Product Market Fit)**.
- Double the investment and optimize that channel: automate, scale and measure LTV/CAC.
- This becomes your **core acquisition channel** for the coming months.

📍 *Example:* You find that your best CAC→LTV comes from LinkedIn outbound and customer referrals → you concentrate on those two.

## 🧭 B2B – Acquisition channels for startups that sell to companies

*Goal: generate qualified leads and turn them into demos or contracts.*

| Channel | Description | Type of experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1️⃣ **Outbound SDR (LinkedIn + Email + WhatsApp)** | Actively reaching decision-makers (Sales Navigator, Apollo, Lemlist). | A sequence of 5 personalized messages → measure reply and demo rates. |
| 2️⃣ **Strategic partnerships** | Alliances with complementary companies (ERP, HRTech, related SaaS). | A co-hosted webinar or joint bundle, tracking shared leads. |
| 3️⃣ **B2B events and trade shows** | Taking part in fairs, meetups or industry associations. | A small stand + an acquisition test (QR to a landing page with a specific offer). |
| 4️⃣ **Thought leadership content (LinkedIn/Blog)** | Publishing technical insights or real cases. | Measure profile visits and the % of inbound messages generated. |
| 5️⃣ **Community-led growth** | Building a community or WhatsApp/Slack group for professionals in the sector. | Launch a channel with 50 members and measure content NPS. |
| 6️⃣ **Account-Based Marketing (ABM)** | Targeting 10–20 companies with personalized campaigns. | Run campaigns by company name and measure engagement and demos. |
| 7️⃣ **PR in specialized media** | Interviews or pieces on innovation in your sector. | Traffic + mentions + inbound leads after publication. |
| 8️⃣ **Cold video outreach** | Personalized 30-second videos via Loom or Vidyard. | Reply rate versus plain text. |
| 9️⃣ **B2B referral programs** | Encouraging current customers to recommend other businesses. | % of leads from referrals and their conversion rate. |
| 🔟 **Highly segmented LinkedIn Ads** | Campaigns for micro-segments (industry, role, company). | Measure CPL (cost per lead) against expected CAC. |

## 🌍 B2C – Acquisition channels for consumer startups

*Goal: maximize reach, conversion and early retention.*

| Channel | Description | Type of experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1️⃣ **Influencer marketing (micro and nano)** | Collaborations with niche creators (1K–10K followers). | 5 posts + conversion tracking by code or link. |
| 2️⃣ **UGC (user-generated content)** | Reposting real user experiences. | Encourage 10 users to share a video and measure engagement. |
| 3️⃣ **Referral programs (Give/Get)** | “Invite a friend and you both get X.” | CTR and new users per active user. |
| 4️⃣ **WhatsApp marketing** | Broadcast lists or chatbots with promotions and launches. | CTR, reply rate and conversion to purchase. |
| 5️⃣ **Events or physical activations** | Pop-ups, fairs or brand activations. | Leads generated + conversion to app or landing page. |
| 6️⃣ **SEO + educational content** | Blogs, videos or mini guides with high search intent. | Organic conversions by topic. |
| 7️⃣ **Email lifecycle (onboarding + retention)** | A welcome sequence with a measurable CTA. | % of active users after email #3. |
| 8️⃣ **Community marketing (Discord, Telegram)** | Building a tribe around the product. | Weekly activity and organic referrals. |
| 9️⃣ **Affiliates or review creators** | Niche bloggers or YouTube channels. | ROI per affiliate (% commission versus LTV). |
| 🔟 **Organic TikTok/YouTube Shorts** | Snackable content showing real use cases. | % of traffic from video → download → sign-up. |

## 🔁 B2B2C – Channels for startups that connect businesses with end users

*Goal: activate both sides of the marketplace at the same time.*

| Channel | Description | Type of experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1️⃣ **Ambassador / reseller model** | Recruiting micro-distributors or social sellers. | Number of sales per ambassador and retention rate. |
| 2️⃣ **Joint onboarding programs** | Training or benefits for the business and its customers. | Number of end customers activated per business. |
| 3️⃣ **Co-branded promotions** | Campaigns with partner brands (for example, producers + retailers). | Measure cross-traffic and joint sales. |
| 4️⃣ **Digital cross-selling** | Advertising in-app or on the partner's dashboard. | CTR + conversions. |
| 5️⃣ **Localized growth campaigns** | Events in specific cities to recruit both sides. | Cost per active business versus cost per active user. |
| 6️⃣ **Mutual referrals** | Consumers recommend new businesses, and the other way around. | Conversion rate of both flows. |
| 7️⃣ **Data-driven outbound** | Using consumption or behavior data to personalize outreach. | Reply and conversion rates. |
| 8️⃣ **Partnerships with institutions or chambers** | Bringing in business clusters or industry associations. | Number of new businesses affiliated and users generated. |
| 9️⃣ **Two-sided social proof** | Success stories of a user and a provider together. | Engagement and inbound leads. |
| 🔟 **Cross incentives (cashback / credits)** | Rewards for both sides of the transaction. | Total acquisition cost divided between both parties. |

## 💡 Bonus tip from a growth expert

Before you spend any money, define for each channel:

1. **Hypothesis** → Why do you think it will work?
2. **Success metric** → Which variable validates the experiment?
3. **Duration** → 7 to 14 days.
4. **Budget** → no more than 5–10% of MRR.
5. **Learning** → What did you discover, even if it failed?
