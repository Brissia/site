This is where you bring all your growth experiments together and make their progress visible, from idea to result. It lets you:

- Prioritize by impact and ease (ICE Scoring or PIE).
- Filter by funnel stage (A – Awareness, A – Acquisition, A – Activation, R – Retention, R – Revenue, R – Referral).
- Measure results and capture the key learnings.
- Make ownership and accountability clear across the team.

## What each experiment records

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Experiment name | Free text |
| Funnel stage | Awareness, Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, Referral |
| Type | Paid, Organic, Owned, Other |
| Status | Not started, In progress, Done |
| Due date | Date |
| Owner | Who answers for the experiment |
| Result | Validated, Not validated |
